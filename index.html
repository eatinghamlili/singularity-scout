Match predictor

Every team gets a strength score that starts from how they've scored this season and updates after each match they play here, with a nudge from our scouts and a few other signals. An alliance's predicted score is its teams' strengths added up, and the bigger the gap between the two alliances, the surer the call. Played matches show ✓ or ✗ so you can see how often it's right.

How to use it

  1. Load from FTCScout to pull a competition's teams and OPR.
  2. To rate a team or add a note, tap its number on the Pick list, Rankings or Quals. Add a note each time you watch it, and rate it 1–10. Ratings from everyone are averaged. Scouting notes opens a read-only summary.
  3. During selection, tap Taken as captains pick, and Pick on your invite.
